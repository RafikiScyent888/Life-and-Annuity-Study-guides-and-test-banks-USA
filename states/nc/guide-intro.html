The North Carolina exams

North Carolina gives separate Life Agent and Accident & Health or Sickness Agent exams. Each has 55 scored, four-option multiple-choice questions plus up to 5 unscored pretest questions. Sections below follow the NC Department of Insurance content outlines (effective March 15, 2024).

Life Agent examScored questions
Types of individual life insurance17
Annuities5
Policy provisions, options and other features11
Other life topics8
NC statutes and regulations pertinent to life14
Accident & Health examScored questions
Accident & health insurance policies22
Accident & health insurance provisions11
Other accident & health topics8
NC statutes and regulations pertinent to accident & health14

About a quarter of each exam is North Carolina law. The rest is covered by the life and health topics that follow.
